# Revised city direction

Use hub-city-boroughs-concept.png as the current scale, topography and composition
reference. It supersedes the earlier compact plaza footprint. The previous
BUILD_REFERENCE.md describes that earlier small layout, not this expanded city.

The market is one recognizable neighborhood within a much larger city.
Hillside housing, bridge districts, working canal basins, upper fortified
terraces and riverward neighborhoods form overlapping urban areas. Streets and
buildings continue beyond the frame; roads, farmland and settlements place the
city in a larger world.

Build around a small number of readable major routes, with many secondary loops
and partly hidden alleys. Canal crossings, gate towers and the fountain establish
landmarks. Make borough identities through elevation, density, street pattern,
planting and working uses rather than a different architectural kit per district.

This is an image-generated environment concept using the actual modular kit as
visual reference, not a measured layout or a render of an assembled Blender city.
The exact routes, stair landings, bridge grades and water levels need a blockout.
Boats and working canal locks shown in the concept are additional set pieces to
model if retained; they are not part of the existing component inventories.
