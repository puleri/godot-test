# Borough architecture and landmarks

Current concept: hub-city-diverse-boroughs.png. This adds new architecture to
the previous city design; these additions are concept designs, not yet modeled
assets in either Blender library.

## Four repeatable building families

- Canal warehouses: long brick masses, broad shallow charcoal roofs, loading
  arches, hoists and working quays. Right-hand basin and foreground canal edge.
- Merchant shop-houses: narrow attached buildings, 3–4 stories, stepped gables,
  colored plaster and arcaded shops. Middle-left canal and market approaches.
- Courtyard tenements: irregular limewashed blocks, exposed brick repairs,
  terracotta roofs, timber galleries and shared courts. Foreground left slope.
- Hillside villas: pale stone, low green-gray hipped roofs, arched loggias and
  planted terraces. Upper-right borough below the defensive wall.

## Three unique landmarks

1. Clock Hall: red-brick civic hall behind the market, a tall clock tower and
   copper spire. Central city orientation point.
2. Canal Bathhouse: pale octagonal quayside building with a turquoise dome and
   steam. Right basin orientation point.
3. Great Waterwheel Mill: asymmetric stone-and-timber workshop, giant wheel and
   brick chimney at the foreground water-level drop. Lower-city orientation point.

Keep each landmark unique. Repeat building families in borough clusters with
mixed transition streets. Preserve the existing timber-and-stone houses as one
vernacular among several. Exact access, bridge grades and water mechanics still
require spatial blockout; this image communicates architectural identity.
